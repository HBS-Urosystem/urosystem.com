    text: >-
      <p>Founded in 2016 in Hungary, Urosystem develops a portfolio of medical
      devices, medications, and diagnostic methods focused on lower urinary
      tract symptoms (LUTS), including interstitial cystitis/bladder pain
      syndrome (IC/BPS) and recurrent urinary tract infections (rUTIs).</p>

      <p>Our mission is to make it easier to diagnose, treat, and follow up on
      LUTS — supporting both patients and healthcare professionals in everyday
      practice.</p>

      <p>Our goal is to improve quality of life for people living with bladder
      or urethral disease worldwide through clinical quality, empathy, and
      science-led innovation.</p>
